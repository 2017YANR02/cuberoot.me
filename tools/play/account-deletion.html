CubeRoot account deletion / CubeRoot 账号注销

Request account deletion

  1. Open CubeRoot account deletion and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open account settings, then choose Delete account.
  3. Review the deletion summary, complete the displayed confirmation, and choose Permanently delete account.

Deletion is immediate, cannot be undone, and has no recovery period.

Data deleted

  • Sign-in identities and account credentials.
  • Friend conversations and related notifications.
  • Cloud timer backups, training results, algorithm progress, followed competitions, scramble markers, drawings, notification settings, support conversations, and private or unlisted reconstructions.
  • Push-device bindings associated with the account.

Data retained or anonymized

Public forum topics, replies, comments, algorithm submissions, and public reconstructions remain available so public discussions and shared links do not break, but they are detached from the account and shown as created by a deleted user. Transaction records may be retained when necessary for accounting, dispute handling, and fraud prevention. Store subscriptions are not canceled by deleting a CubeRoot account; cancel an applicable subscription in the store first.

Local timer data is stored on the device. Delete individual solves in the app, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove all local app data.

申请注销账号

  1. 打开 CubeRoot 账号注销页面，登录需要注销的账号。
  2. 进入账号设置，选择注销账号。
  3. 阅读删除说明，完成页面要求的确认，然后选择永久注销账号。

注销立即生效，无法撤销，也没有恢复期。

会删除的数据

  • 登录身份和账号凭据。
  • 好友聊天及相关通知。
  • 计时器云备份、训练成绩、公式进度、关注的比赛、打乱标记、画板作品、通知设置、反馈会话及私享或不公开列出的复盘。
  • 与账号关联的推送设备绑定。

会保留或匿名化的数据

为避免公开讨论和分享链接断开，公开论坛主题、回帖、评论、公式投稿和公开复盘会继续保留，但会解除账号关联并显示为“已注销用户”。为对账、处理争议和防止欺诈，必要的交易记录可能继续保留。注销 CubeRoot 账号不会取消适用的商店订阅，请先在对应商店中取消。

本地计时数据保存在设备上。你可以在 App 中删除单条成绩，或清除 App 存储、卸载 App，以删除全部本地 App 数据。
