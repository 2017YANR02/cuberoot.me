| [3h+](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#3h+) | [CLARIFICATION] Puzzles may be refined internally by sanding, lubrication, or modifications which improve stability (e.g. magnets). | [澄清] 魔方内部可以被精心打磨、润滑或者修改以提高稳定性（例如加磁）。 |

| [3h1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#3h1) | "Pillowed" puzzles are permitted. | 可以使用“面包”魔方。 |

| [4a1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#4a1) | Exception: for extra attempts for 5x5x5 Cube, 6x6x6 Cube, 7x7x7 Cube, and Megaminx, the scramble sequence may be applied to an unsolved puzzle, at the discretion of the WCA Delegate. This exception only applies to scramble sequences for extra attempts. When applying a scramble sequence to an unsolved puzzle, the scramble may begin in any orientation, the usual requirements in [Regulation 4d](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#4d) do not apply. | 例外：对于五阶、六阶、七阶魔方及五魔方，额外尝试用的备用打乱可以在未复原的魔方上使用，由WCA代表决定。这个例外只对额外尝试的打乱有效。当使用未复原的魔方进行打乱时，打乱可以由任意朝向开始，不必遵循[规则4d](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#4d)中的通常要求。 |

| [4b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#4b) | Competition scramble sequences must be generated using a current official version of an official WCA scramble program (available [via the WCA website](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/regulations/scrambles/)). | 比赛打乱公式必须由当前版本的WCA官方打乱程序（可在[WCA网站上](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/regulations/scrambles/)获得）生成。 |

| [4b2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#4b2) | Scramble sequences for a group must be available only to the WCA Delegate before the start of that group, and only available to the WCA Delegate and the scramblers for the group until it is finished. | [4b2++](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#4b2++) | [ADDITION] Competition organizers should ensure that scramblers, scramble sequences, and partially/fully scrambled puzzles are visually isolated from competitors (see [Regulation A2c](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A2c)). ## Article 5: Puzzle Defects / 第五章：魔方故障

| 条款 / Clause | English | 简体中文 / Chinese |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [5a](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5a) | Examples of puzzle defects include: popped parts, pieces twisted in place, and detached screws/caps/stickers. | 魔方故障的例子包括：POP，某些块原地转，掉贴纸、贴片、盖子等。 |
| [5b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b) | If a puzzle defect occurs during an attempt, the competitor may choose to either repair the defect and continue the attempt, or to stop the attempt. | 如果在某次还原中发生了魔方故障，选手可以选择修复魔方继续还原，或是停止还原。 |
| [5b1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b1) | If a competitor chooses to repair the puzzle, they must repair only the defective parts. Tools and/or parts of other puzzles must not be used to repair the original puzzle. Penalty: disqualification of the attempt (DNF). | 如果选手选择修复魔方，他只能修复发生故障的块，其他块或工具禁止被用于修复。惩罚：取消当前还原成绩（DNF）。 |
| [5b2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b2) | Any repair to a puzzle must not give the competitor an advantage in solving the puzzle. Penalty: disqualification of the attempt (DNF). | 对魔方的修复禁止给还原带来任何优势。惩罚：取消当前还原成绩（DNF）。 |
| [5b3](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b3) | Permitted repairs: | 允许的修复： |
| [5b3a](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b3a) | If any parts have fallen out or moved out of place, the competitor may place them back. | 如果任何块掉出了它原有的位置，选手可以将它装回来。 |
| [5b3b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b3b) | If the competitor repairs the puzzle but finds that the puzzle is unsolvable later during the solve, they may disassemble and reassemble a maximum of 4 pieces to make the puzzle solvable. | 如果选手修复魔方后在还原过程中发现魔方变得不可还原，他可以重新拆下并装上至多4个块使得魔方可被还原。 |
| [5b3c](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b3c) | If the puzzle is unsolvable, and can be made solvable by rotating a single corner piece, the competitor may correct the corner piece by twisting it in place without disassembling the puzzle. If multiple corner pieces have been twisted as a result of puzzle defects, the competitor may correct these twists, as long as the number of pieces corrected by the competitor does not exceed the number of pieces twisted as a result of puzzle defects. | 如果魔方无法还原，但通过旋转单个角块即可使其可还原，选手可以通过原地旋转角块来修正，而无需拆装魔方。如果由于魔方故障导致多个角块发生了旋转，选手可以纠正这些旋转，但前提是选手纠正的角块数量不得超过因魔方故障而旋转的角块数量。 |
| [5b4](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b4) | During a blindfolded phase (see [Regulation B4](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#B4)), all repairs must be performed blindfolded. Penalty: disqualification of the attempt (DNF). | 在盲拧阶段（见[规则B4](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#B4)），所有修复必须盲进行。惩罚：取消当前还原成绩（DNF）。 |
| [5b5](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5) | If some parts of the puzzle are physically detached or not fully placed at the end of the solve, the following regulations apply: | 如果在结束还原时魔方的一些部分掉出或没有完全归位，则按照参考如下规则判定： |

| [5b5++](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5++) | [EXAMPLE] Examples of parts physically detached, resulting in a DNF: two 3x3x3 center caps, two big cube center pieces, a 3x3x3 edge piece, a 4x4x4 edge piece ("wing"), any combination of two pieces that both have colored parts. | [举例] 某些块飞出，判定为DNF的例子：三阶魔方的两个中心盖，两个高阶魔方的中心块，三阶魔方的一个棱块，四阶魔方的一个棱块，含有颜色的两个块的任意组合。 |
| [5b5a](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5a) | If one or more parts without colored faces are affected, the puzzle is considered solved. | 一个或多个不含颜色块的部分受影响的情况，则判定为正常还原。 |
| [5b5b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5b) | If one part with one colored face is affected, the puzzle is considered solved. | 只有一个只含单颜色块的部分受影响，则判定为正常还原。 |
| [5b5c](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5c) | If more than one part with one colored face is affected, the puzzle is considered unsolved (DNF). | 两个及以上只含单颜色块部分受影响，则判定为未还原（DNF）。 |
| [5b5d](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5d) | If one or more parts with more than one colored face are affected, the puzzle is considered unsolved (DNF). | 一个及以上含多种颜色块的部分受影响，则判定为未还原（DNF）。 |
| [5b5e](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5e) | Regulations [5b5c](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5c) and [5b5d](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5d) supersede [5b5a](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5a) and [5b5b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5b). | [规则5b5c](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#5b5c)与[规则5b5d](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#5b5d)比[规则5b5a](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#5b5a)与[规则5b5b](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#5b5b)优先判定. |
| [5b5f](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5f) | If a part is partially detached or not fully placed, its final position is the closest position in which the affected piece would normally fit within the puzzle mechanism, considering the current state of the puzzle. If the final position renders the piece solved, the piece is not considered to be affected by the puzzle defect. | 如果一个魔方的部件部分掉出或没有完全归位，其最终位置由按照魔方结构通常可以安放的最近位置决定，并参考魔方当前的状态。如果最终位置显示该块已经复原，则该块被认为没有受到魔方故障的影响。 |
| [5b5f+](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5f+) | [EXAMPLE] There is a [Visual Guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/15XszaCGNvy3Dk6X6qERzZWZaDH1RH04z/view) available for [Regulation 5b5f](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5f). | [举例] 对于[规则5b5f](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#5b5f)，这里有一个[可视化指南](https://drive.google.com/file/d/15XszaCGNvy3Dk6X6qERzZWZaDH1RH04z/view)。 |
| [5b5f++](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5f++) | [EXAMPLE] A 4x4x4 edge piece ("wing") twisted in place is considered solved. | [举例] 一个四阶的翼棱在它自己的位置翻转的状态被认为是还原的。 |
| [5b5f+++](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5f+++) | [EXAMPLE] A corner affected by a corner twist is considered to be placed in a valid position, but such position does not render the piece solved. | [举例] 一个旋转了的角块被认为是在它自己的位置，但这样的状态不能得出这块是复原的。 |
| [5b5f++++](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b5f++++) | [CLARIFICATION] If there are multiple pieces partially detached or not fully placed, their final position is assessed one at a time. The piece that is closest to an open position is assessed first, and is then considered to occupy that position. This process is repeated until all partially detached or not fully placed pieces have been assessed. | [澄清] 如果有多个部分脱落或未完全归位的部件，其最终位置应逐一进行评估。最接近一个空位的部件优先被评估，并被视为占据该位置。重复此过程直到所有部分脱落或未完全归位的部件都被评估完成。 |
| [5b6](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b6) | Definition: A part is one or more fully connected components from a group of puzzle components that remain connected and do not move relative to each other during ordinary operation of the puzzle (e.g. a 3x3x3 center cap, a Pyraminx tip, a 3x3x3 corner piece missing one face, one face of a 3x3x3 corner piece). | 定义：一个“部分”指的是由一个或多个零件组成的、在常规操作中不会相对移动的、保持连接在一起的整体(例如：三阶魔方的中心盖、金字塔的小角、缺少一个面的三阶魔方的角块、三阶魔方的角块的一个面等)。 |
| [5c+](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5c+) | [REMINDER] If a competitor has a puzzle defect, this does not grant them the right to an extra attempt. | [提醒] 魔方发生故障不能作为选手获得额外还原机会的理由。 |
| [5d](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5d) | The competitor is not permitted to cause a puzzle defect intentionally. Examples of intentionally causing puzzle defects: removing an attached center cap, rotating a corner piece, peeling a sticker. | 选手不允许故意制造魔方故障。 故意制造魔方故障的例子：取下中心盖、旋转角块、撕下贴纸。 |
| [5d1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5d1) | Exception: the competitor may intentionally cause a puzzle defect while repairing a previous puzzle defect (see [Regulation 5b3b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b3b) and [Regulation 5b3c](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b3c)). | 例外：选手在修复之前的故障的时候（见[规则5b3b](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#5b3b)和[规则5b3c](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#5b3c)）可以故意制造魔方故障。 |
| [5d2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5d2) | Exception: if a competitor incorrectly fixes an existing puzzle defect, for example by rotating a corner piece in the wrong direction (see [Regulation 5b3c](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5b3c)), this is not considered intentionally causing a puzzle defect. | 例外：如果选手没有正确修复故障，例如向反方向旋转角块（见[规则5b3c](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#5b3c)），这种情况不被视为故意制造魔方故障。 |
| [5d3](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#5d3) | Penalty for intentionally causing a puzzle defect: disqualification of the attempt (DNF). | 对故意制造魔方故障的惩罚：取消当前还原成绩（DNF）。 |

| [A1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A1) | Speed Solving attempts must abide by the following procedures. | 速拧还原必须遵循以下过程。 |
| [A1a](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A1a) | The organization team must apply at least one kind of time limit (time limit per attempt and/or cumulative time limit) to each round. | 主办团队必须对单次还原或轮次加上至少一种时限（单次还原的时限和/或累计还原时限）。 |
| [A1a+](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A1a+) | [CLARIFICATION] If both limits are applied, the time limit per attempt must not be greater than the cumulative time limit. | [澄清] 如果两种时限都使用了，单次还原的时限不得比累计还原时限更长。 |
| [A1a1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A1a1) | The default time limit is 10 minutes for each attempt in a round, though the organization team may announce a higher or lower time limit per attempt. In this case, the limit must be the same for all attempts in a round. | 一轮比赛种的单次还原的默认时限为10分钟，主办团队也可以宣布一个更长或更短的单次还原时限。这种情况下，一轮比赛中的每一次还原的时限必须相同。 |
| [A1a2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A1a2) | For events other than 3x3x3 Fewest Moves and 3x3x3 Multi-Blind, the organization team may define a cumulative time limit. The cumulative time limit must either apply to all attempts in one round (e.g. 3 attempts with a cumulative time limit of 20 minutes), to all attempts in Dual Rounds for one event (see [Regulation 9v](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#9v)), or to all attempts in a combination of rounds for different events. In this case, the time limit for a particular attempt is the time limit or the cumulative time limit minus the total time in other attempts counting towards the time limit so far (see [Regulation A1a5](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A1a5)), whichever is lower. If a round has an applicable cumulative time limit, it must have at most one. | 除三阶最少步和三阶多盲外的项目，主办团队也可以采用累计时限。累计还原时限必须应用在一轮中的所有尝试（例如3次还原的累计时间不超过20分钟），或者应用于在一个项目的双重轮中的所有尝试（见[规则9v](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#9v)），亦或应用于不同项目的轮次组合中的所有尝试。在这种情况下，某一次特定的还原的时限是它自己的时限或者总的还原时限减去到目前为止已经消耗的时限（见[规则A1a5](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#A1a5)），以更短的为准。如果一个轮次有适用的累计还原时限，它最多只能有一个。 |

| [A6b1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A6b1) | If the timer stops before the competitor has solved the puzzle and the timer shows a time strictly below 0.06 seconds, then the attempt is replaced by an extra attempt. | [A6b1+](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A6b1+) | [EXPLANATION] The arbitrary value of 0.06 seconds was chosen to accommodate concerns about speedsolving timer malfunctions. | [解释] 刻意选择0.06秒是为了容纳计时器故障。 |
| [A6b2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A6b2) | If the timer stops before the competitor has solved the puzzle and displays a time of 0.06 seconds or higher, then the attempt is disqualified (DNF). Exception: if the judge is unable to determine whether any move or any change in alignment has been applied after stopping the timer and before releasing the puzzle, the attempt may be disqualified (DNF), at the discretion of the judge. | 选手在停止计时器前必须完全放下魔方。惩罚：加罚时间（+2秒）。例外：如果在停止计时器之后，放下魔方之前裁判无法判断有没有转动魔方或者改变魔方错位程度的情况，可以选择取消此次还原成绩（DNF），由裁判决定。 |
| [A6d](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A6d) | The competitor must stop the speedsolving timer using both hands, placed flat on the sensors with palms down. Penalty: time penalty (+2 seconds). | 选手必须使用双手，手心向下平放于计时器感应区以停止计时器。惩罚：加罚时间（+2秒）。 |

| [A6e+](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A6e+) | [CLARIFICATION] If the puzzle falls into the lap of the competitor after stopping the timer, this is considered touching the puzzle. | [澄清] 如果魔方在计时器停止之后掉落到选手的大腿上，这种情况被视为触碰魔方。 |

| [A6e1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A6e1) | Penalty for applying a move to the puzzle after stopping the timer: disqualification of the attempt (DNF). | 停止计时器后转动魔方的惩罚：取消此次还原资格（DNF）。 |
| [A6e2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A6e2) | Penalty for touching the puzzle after stopping the timer without applying any moves: time penalty (+2 seconds). | [A6f](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A6f) | The competitor must not reset the timer until both the competitor and the judge have signed the score sheet. Exception: see [Regulation A7c5](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A7c5). | 在选手和裁判都签名完成之前选手禁止重置计时器。惩罚：取消此次还原资格（DNF），由WCA代表决定。例外：见[规则A7c5](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#A7c5). |

| [A7](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A7) | Recording results: | 记录成绩： |

| [A7a1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A7a1) | The judge should indicate to the competitor that the puzzle is solved, or that a penalty will be applied. | 裁判应该告诉选手魔方已还原，或者任何应有的惩罚。 |

| [A7b1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A7b1) | A properly formatted time follows the format "h:mm:ss.cc", where "h" represents hours, "mm" represents minutes, "ss" represents seconds and "cc" represents centiseconds. Minor variations to the results format in line with regional practice (e.g. using a comma instead of a period, using "60:00.00" instead of "1:00:00.00") may be permitted at the discretion of the WCA Delegate. | 正确格式的时间应记为“h:mm:ss.cc”，其中“h”表示小时，“mm”表示分钟，“ss”表示秒，“cc”表示百分秒。对于十分钟及以上的时间，以及所有三阶多盲的时间，可以省略百分秒（见[规则9f2](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#9f2)）。在符合地区习惯的情况下，对成绩格式的轻微调整（例如使用逗号代替小数点，使用“60:00.00”代替“1:00:00.00”）可由WCA代表酌情允许。 |

| [H1a2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H1a2) | A competitor may request to keep their submission private until all competitors have submitted their numbers. Once all competitors have reported their numbers, the information becomes public (e.g. a competitor may ask for the numbers of any other competitors before starting their attempt). | 选手可以要求将提交的还原个数保密，直到所有选手都提交了还原个数。一旦所有选手都完成提交，信息将被公开（例如某个选手可以在他开始还原前询问任何其他选手的个数）。 |
| [H1a3](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H1a3) | Before the attempt, the scrambled puzzles must be placed on the table in an arbitrary orientation while ensuring they remain completely covered. The puzzles should be arranged in a shape as square as possible and should not be stacked (e.g. 8 puzzles should be arranged in 2 rows with 3 puzzles and 1 row with 2 puzzles and in 1 layer only). | 开始还原前，打乱好的魔方必须朝向随机地被放在桌上，并保证被遮挡住。魔方应该被摆放成尽量方形并且不应该堆叠起来（例如：8个魔方应该被摆放成2行3个魔方以及一行两个魔方，并被放置成一层）。 |
| [H1b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H1b) | If a competitor is attempting fewer than 6 puzzles, they are allotted a time limit of 10 minutes times the number of puzzles in the attempt, else the time limit is 60 minutes. Exception: a competitor who arrives late may compete with a reduced time limit, at the discretion of the WCA Delegate. This competitor's attempt must not be scrambled using the same scramble sequences as competitors who have already completed their attempt (see [Regulation H1f](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H1f)). | 如果某个选手尝试还原小于6个魔方，则他的还原时限是10分钟乘以还原个数，否则时限为60分钟。例外：迟到的裁判可以在更短的时限内参与比赛，由WCA代表决定。此选手的尝试禁止使用和已完成尝试的选手相同的打乱（见[规则H1f](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#H1f)）。 |
| [H1b1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H1b1) | The competitor may signal the end of the solve at any time. If and when the time limit is reached, the judge stops the attempt and the attempt is then scored; the time limit for the attempt counts as the original recorded time. | 选手可以在还原的任何时候示意结束还原。如果达到时限，裁判停止选手的还原并记录成绩，还原时间记为时限。 |
| [H1b1+](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H1b1+) | [REMINDER] The attempt is not disqualified for reaching the time limit, due to exceptions for 3x3x3 Multi-Blind (see [Regulation A1a4](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A1a4) and [Regulation A1a5](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A1a5)). | [提醒] 到达时限后，整个尝试不会被取消成绩（DNF），由三阶多盲规则的例外限定（见[规则A1a4](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#A1a4)和[规则A1a5](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#A1a5)）。 |
| [H1d](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H1d) | Time penalties for the puzzles of the attempt are cumulative. | 所有魔方的加罚时间累加。 |
| [H1d+](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H1d+) | [EXAMPLE] Example: Suppose a competitor attempts 10 cubes, stops the solve with a time of 59:57, and has two time penalties. The time of the final result is 59:57 + 2*2 = 60:01 (also see [Regulation A1a5](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A1a5)). | [举例] 举例：如果选手尝试10个魔方，在59：57秒结束还原，并且有两次加罚时间，那么最终成绩的时间是59：57 + 2*2 = 60:01（或见[规则A1a5](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#A1a5)） |
| [H1d++](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H1d++) | [EXAMPLE] Example: Suppose a competitor attempts 10 cubes, the judge stops the competitor at 60 minutes, and the attempt has two time penalties. The time of the final result is 60:00 + 2*2 = 60:04. | [举例] 举例：如果选手尝试10个魔方，在60分的时候裁判由于超时结束该选手的还原，并且这些还原中有两次加罚时间，那么最终的时间是60:00 + 2*2 = 60:04。 |
| [H1e](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H1e) | Any disqualification penalty during an attempt applies to the entire attempt (DNF). Exception: for penalties relating to individual puzzles (e.g. making a move during memorization, using an illegal puzzle), those puzzles may be considered unsolved at the end of the attempt instead of disqualifying the entire attempt, at the discretion of the WCA Delegate. | 在尝试中任何导致取消成绩的判罚都将导致整个尝试被取消成绩（DNF）。例外：对于仅和单个魔方相关的惩罚（例如在记忆过程转动魔方、使用不合规的魔方等），这些魔方可以在尝试结束时被算作未还原，而不是取消整个尝试的成绩，由WCA代表决定。 |
| [H1f](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H1f) | If it is planned for competitors to start at significantly different times such that there is a risk that information about the scrambles may have become known to competitors, attempts starting later must use different scramble sequences. | 如果计划让选手在明显不同的时间开始比赛，且存在选手获知打乱序列的风险，则后开始的尝试必须使用不同的打乱序列。 |
| [H2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H2) | The competitor may self supply a flat covering (e.g. a mat or a cloth) for the surface, at the discretion of the WCA Delegate. If the competitor wishes to do so, the request must be made at the same time as submitting the number of puzzles (see [Regulation H1a](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#H1a)). | 选手可以选择自备一个平整的遮盖物（例如一张垫子或者布料）来遮盖魔方，由WCA代表决定。如果选手想这么做，则他必须在提交魔方数量的时候提出（见[规则H1a](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#H1a)） |

## Article I: Head to Head Solving / 附则I: “一对一”复原

| 条款 / Clause | English | 简体中文 / Chinese |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [I1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I1) | Qualification and seeding: | 资格和种子顺位： |
| [I1a](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I1a) | The number of available places in a Head to Head round must be one of the following: 4, 8, 12, or 16. | “一对一”轮次的人数必须是这几个之一：4、8、12或16。 |
| [I1b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I1b) | For Head to Head rounds with a prior round, seeding is based on the competitor's rank in the prior round. | 在有前序轮次的“一对一”轮次中，种子顺位依据选手在前一轮中的排名决定。 |
| [I1c](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I1c) | For Head to Head round without a prior round, seeding is based on the qualification requirements for the competition. | 在没有前序轮次的“一对一”轮次中，种子顺位依据比赛的参赛资格要求决定。 |
| [I1d](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I1d) | If competitors would otherwise have identical seeds, seeding between these competitors is randomized. | 如果多位选手会获得相同的种子顺位，则这些选手之间的种子顺位以随机排序决定。 |
| [I2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2) | Round structure: | 轮次结构： |
| [I2a](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2a) | A Head to Head round is structured as a single-elimination tournament, comprising a number of stages: | “一对一”轮次由几个阶段的单败淘汰赛制组成： |
| [I2a1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2a1) | For rounds with 4 places: Semifinal Stage and Final Stage. | 对于有4名选手的情况：半决赛阶段和决赛阶段。 |
| [I2a2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2a2) | For rounds with 8 places: Quarterfinal Stage, Semifinal Stage, and Final Stage. | 对于有8名选手的情况：四分之一决赛阶段、半决赛阶段以及决赛阶段。 |
| [I2a3](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2a3) | For rounds with 12 places: Stage of 12, Quarterfinal Stage, Semifinal Stage, and Final Stage. | 对于有12名选手的情况：前十二阶段、四分之一决赛阶段、半决赛阶段以及决赛阶段。 |
| [I2a4](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2a4) | For rounds with 16 places: Stage of 16, Quarterfinal Stage, Semifinal Stage, and Final Stage. | 对于有16名选手的情况：前十六阶段、四分之一决赛阶段、半决赛阶段以及决赛阶段。 |
| [I2b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2b) | Each stage comprises a number of matches between 2 competitors: | 每个阶段由若干在2名选手之间进行的对决构成： |
| [I2b1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2b1) | Final Stage: 2 Matches: the Final Match and the Third Place Match. | 决赛阶段：2场对决：决赛和三四名决赛。 |
| [I2b2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2b2) | Semifinal Stage: 2 matches. | 半决赛阶段：2场对决。 |
| [I2b3](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2b3) | Quarterfinal Stage: 4 matches. | 四分之一决赛阶段：4场对决。 |
| [I2b4](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2b4) | Stage of 12: 4 matches. | 前十二阶段：4场对决。 |
| [I2b5](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2b5) | Stage of 16: 8 matches. | 前十六阶段：8场对决。 |
| [I2c](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2c) | Matchups in the first stage of a round are based on a competitor's seed. The competitor with the highest seed is matched against the competitor with the lowest seed. The competitor with the second highest seed is matched against the competitor with the second lowest seed. This process is repeated until all competitors have been matched. | 第一阶段的对阵由选手的种子顺位决定。最高种子对阵最低种子。第二高种子对阵第二低种子。这样一直到所有选手的对阵安排结束。 |
| [I2c1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2c1) | Exception: for rounds with 12 places the top 4 seeded competitors do not take part in the Stage of 12, but are matched against the winners of the Stage of 12 during the Quarterfinal Stage. | 例外：对于有12名选手的阶段，前4的种子选手不参与前十二阶段，而是和前十二阶段的胜者在四分之一决赛阶段对阵。 |
| [I2d](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2d) | The winner of each match during the Stage of 16, Stage of 12 and Quarterfinal Stage proceeds to the following stage. The loser is eliminated. | 前十六阶段、前十二阶段、和四分之一决赛阶段的每场对决的胜者晋级到下一阶段。败者出局。 |
| [I2e](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2e) | The winner of each match during the Semifinal Stage takes part in the Final Match during the Final Stage. The loser of each match during the Semifinal Stage takes part in the Third Place Match during the Final Stage. | 半决赛阶段的每场对决的胜者参与决赛阶段的决赛。半决赛阶段的每场对决的败者参与决赛阶段的三四名决赛。 |
| [I2f](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2f) | For subsequent stages competitors are matched so that, assuming the higher seed won every previous match, the seeds add up to the number of remaining competitors plus 1. | 后续阶段的对阵按如下规则确定：假设每场对决都由更高顺位的种子胜出，则由种子顺位之和等于剩余人数加1的两位选手互相对阵。 |
| [I2g](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2g) | A [Visual Guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dGy7DZZqPNuf8dDHJIJUw5vzwgoRjz1G/view) illustrates the full structure for rounds with 4, 8, 12, and 16 competitors. | 这里有一个[可视化指南](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dGy7DZZqPNuf8dDHJIJUw5vzwgoRjz1G/view)展示了包含4、8、12以及16人轮次的完整对阵表。 |
| [I2h](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I2h) | If a competitor withdraws from a match before it begins, their opponent is treated as winning the match for the purpose of progression (Stage of 16, Stage of 12, Quarterfinal Stage, Semifinal Stage) or overall ranking within the round (Final Stage), but no results are recorded for the match (see [Regulation 9f5b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#9f5b)). | 如果一名选手在某场对决开始前退出，则他的对手被认为是获得了这场对决的胜利，并晋级到下一个阶段（对于前十六阶段、前十二阶段、四分之一决赛阶段以及半决赛的情况）或者获得相应的排名（对于决赛阶段的情况），但这场比赛没有成绩记录（见[规则9f5b](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#9f5b)）。 |
| [I3](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I3) | Match structure: | 对决结构: |
| [I3a](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I3a) | Each match comprises a number of sets. The winner of the match is the first competitor to win X sets (where X is 1, 2, or 3). | 每场对决由若干盘构成。先赢得X盘（X是1、2或3）的选手赢得对决的胜利。 |
| [I3a1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I3a1) | X must be the same for all matches within a stage. Exception: for the Final Match, X may be greater than for the Third Place Match. | 在同一个阶段，所有的对决必须使用一样的X值。例外：对于决赛，X可以比三四名决赛的值更大。 |
| [I3a2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I3a2) | For subsequent stages, X must be the same or higher than the previous stage. | 后续阶段的X值必须大于或等于前序阶段。 |
| [I3b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I3b) | Each set comprises a number of points. The winner of a set is the first competitor to win 3 points. | 每一盘由若干分组成。先得到3分的选手赢得该盘。 |
| [I3c](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I3c) | The winner of a point is the competitor with the better result, where "better" is defined as the shorter time. A DNF or DNS is the worst possible result. | 每一分成绩更好的选手得到这一分，这里的“好”指的是更短的时间。DNF或DNS是最差的可能成绩。 |
| [I3d](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I3d) | If neither competitor has a better result, neither wins the point. | 如果双方都没有获得更好的成绩，双方均不得分。 |
| [I3e](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I3e) | If neither competitor wins a set after 7 points, the competitor with the most points wins the set. If both competitors have the same number of points, the competitor with the best single result in the set wins the set. If neither competitor has a best single result in the set, the best single results in the match and then single results in the round are considered. If necessary, the competitors' seeds act as a final tiebreaker, with the higher-seeded competitor winning the set. | 7分之后，如果还没有人赢得这一盘，则当前更高分的选手赢得这盘。如果分数相同，则在这盘中获得最好单次成绩的选手赢得这盘。如果还不能区分，则依序比较在当前对决中的最好单次成绩以及在整轮当中的单次最好成绩。如还有必要，种子顺位将作为最后的区分手段，高种子选手赢得该盘。 |
| [I4](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4) | Head to Head speed solving procedures: | “一对一”速拧流程： |
| [I4a](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4a) | Standard speed solving procedures are followed, as described in [Article A](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#article-A). Additional regulations that supersede the corresponding procedures in Article A are described below. | 主要遵循标准速拧流程，如[附则A](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#article-A)中所述。覆盖附则A中相应流程的附加规则如下所述。 |
| [I4b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4b) | Scrambling: | 打乱： |
| [I4b1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4b1) | Competitors in a match must use the same scramble sequences as each other for a point. Each point must use different scramble sequences and each match must use different scramble sequences. | 一场对决中的选手必须在每一分的比赛中都使用相同的打乱序列。不同分必须使用不同的打乱序列，不同对决也必须使用不同的打乱序列。 |
| [I4c](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4c) | Inspection: | 观察： |
| [I4c1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4c1) | When both competitors are present at the solving station and when each judge is ready, the announcer asks "READY?". | 当两名选手均已经到达比赛位并且各裁判已经做好准备，发令员问“准备好了吗？”。 |
| [I4c2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4c2) | When both competitors have confirmed they are ready, or once at least 15 seconds have passed, the announcer calls "START" and starts timing the inspection, beginning the point. | 当两名选手均已确认准备好或者至少已经过了15秒，发令员报“开始观察”并开始对观察进行计时，开始这一分的对决。 |
| [I4c3](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4c3) | When 8 seconds of inspection have elapsed, the announcer calls "8 SECONDS". | 当观察时间达到8秒时，发令员报出“8秒”。 |
| [I4c4](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4c4) | When 11 seconds, 12 seconds, and 13 seconds of inspection have elapsed, the announcer calls "3", "2", and "1" respectively. | 当观察时间达到11秒、12秒、13秒时，发令员依序报出“3”、“2”、“1”。 |
| [I4c5](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4c5) | When 14 seconds of inspection have elapsed, the announcer calls "GO". The competitor must start the solve (see [Regulation A4d](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A4d)) at this point. Penalty for intentionally delaying the start of the solve: disqualification of the attempt (DNF) at the discretion of the judge. | 当观察时间达到14秒时，发令员报出“开始”。选手此时必须开始复原（见[规则A4d](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#A4d)）。对故意延迟开始复原的处罚：取消此次尝试成绩（DNF），由裁判决定。 |
| [I4c6](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4c6) | Due to modified inspection procedures, the usual penalties for not starting within 15 seconds (see [Regulation A4d1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A4d1) and [Regulation A4d2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#A4d2)) do not apply in Head to Head rounds. | 因为观察流程的修改，通常对于没有在15秒内开始的处罚（见[规则A4d1](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#A4d1)和[规则A4d2](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#A4d2)）在“一对一”轮次中不适用。 |
| [I4c7](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4c7) | The competitor must not start the solve before the announcer calls "GO". Penalty: disqualification of the attempt (DNF) at the discretion of the judge. | 选手禁止在发令员报出“开始”之前开始复原。惩罚：取消此次尝试成绩（DNF），由裁判决定。 |
| [I4d](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4d) | Recording results: | 记录成绩: |
| [I4d1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4d1) | When recording the result, the judge also notes whether a competitor won or lost a point, or there was no winner, on the score sheet. | 在记录成绩时，裁判同样也在成绩单上备注这名选手在这一分上是获胜还是落败，或者是没有人获胜。 |
| [I5](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I5) | Head to Head blindfolded solving procedures: | “一对一”盲拧流程： |
| [I5a](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I5a) | Standard speed solving procedures (as described in [Article A](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#article-A)), blindfolded solving procedures (as described in [Article B](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#article-B)) and Head to Head speed solving procedures (as described in [Regulation I4](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I4)) are followed. Additional regulations that supersede the corresponding procedures are described below. | 主要遵循标准速拧流程（如[附则A](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#article-A)中所述）、标准盲拧流程（如[附则B](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#article-B)中所述）以及“一对一”速拧流程（如[规则I4](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#I4)中所述）。覆盖其中相应流程的附加规则如下所述。 |
| [I5b](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I5b) | Starting the attempt: | 开始尝试： |
| [I5b1](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I5b1) | When both competitors are present at the solving station and when each judge is ready, the announcer asks "READY?". | 当两名选手均已经到达比赛位并且各裁判已经做好准备，发令员问“准备好了吗？”。 |
| [I5b2](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I5b2) | When both competitors have confirmed they are ready, or once at least 15 seconds have passed, the announcer calls “3, 2, 1, GO". The competitor must start the solve (see [Regulation B2d](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#B2d)) at this point. Penalty for intentionally delaying the start of the solve: disqualification of the attempt (DNF) at the discretion of the judge. | 当两名选手均已确认准备好或者至少已经过了15秒，发令员报“3、2、1，开始”。选手此时必须开始复原（见[规则B2d](https://cuberoot.me/zh/regulation/full#B2d)）。对故意延迟开始复原的处罚：取消此次尝试成绩（DNF），由裁判决定。 |
| [I5b3](https://cuberoot.me/regulation/full#I5b3) | The competitor must not start the solve before the announcer calls "GO". Penalty: disqualification of the attempt (DNF) at the discretion of the judge. | 选手禁止在发令员报出“开始”之前开始复原。惩罚：取消此次尝试成绩（DNF），由裁判决定。 |
